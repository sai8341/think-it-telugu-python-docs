# Who Should Take This Course?

Whether you want to build your first project, prepare for AI & ML job interviews, or switch to tech from a non-technical background — this course will guide you step by step.

## This Course is Perfect For You If:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Complete Beginners" icon="user-check">
    You have never written a line of code before and want a clear, friendly roadmap starting from absolute zero.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Students & Job Seekers" icon="star">
    You are preparing for AI & ML engineering interviews and want to build real projects that make your resume stand out.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Non-IT Professionals" icon="layers">
    You work in another field (commerce, science, management) and want to learn AI skills or switch your career to AI engineering.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hands-On Learners" icon="code">
    You learn best by typing code, fixing real errors, and building working applications — not by watching boring slides.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Get the Most Out of This Course

1. **Keep Your Code Editor Open:** Whenever you see a code block, type it out yourself in VS Code or our online Python Lab.
2. **Make Mistakes on Purpose:** Try changing numbers, deleting quotes, or changing variable names just to see what errors you get. Understanding errors is the fastest way to learn!
3. **Use the Accordions:** Check the `# Wrong` vs `# Right` boxes at the bottom of each lesson to see common mistakes before you make them.
